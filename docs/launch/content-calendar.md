# 30-day launch calendar

Use “Day 1” as the first day after the production release gate. Do not compress missed days by spamming several communities at once.

## Preflight (before Day 1)

- Confirm production smoke path and operational alerts.
- Leave Dodo in test mode until the separate live-billing checklist.
- Create/complete founder profiles and product profiles; Product Hunt founder account must be at least one week old.
- Prepare the tagged-link sheet and daily dashboard.
- List 30 researched founder prospects and 20 community conversations worth joining.
- Capture real desktop/mobile screenshots and the three short product clips in `creative-briefs.md`.
- Invite no one until the accepted/rejected beta email templates are ready.

## Product Hunt launch-day checklist

### Before 12:01 AM Pacific

- Production status and smoke check green.
- Pricing, Free limits, privacy links, support contact, and demo all accurate.
- Maker profiles connected.
- First maker comment ready.
- UTM destination tested through auth and project creation.
- Team knows severity/rollback ownership.

### During the day

- Reply to questions with specifics, including limitations.
- Track activation by source every four hours, not obsessively every minute.
- Capture repeated confusion in one issue list.
- Share the launch from founder/product accounts without vote manipulation.
- Never offer rewards for votes and never ask for a particular ranking.

### After the day

- Send a product-focused thank-you.
- Fix high-confidence friction before adding features.
- Report visits, projects, verified installs, first feedback, and support load.
- Continue onboarding; the launch is a beginning, not the result.

## Weekly meeting agenda (30 minutes)

1. raw funnel counts by source;
2. three verbatim customer sentences;
3. largest activation loss;
4. reliability/security/support incidents;
5. one message to repeat;
6. one channel to pause;
7. one product fix and one content artifact for next week.

## Contingencies

- **Security/data incident:** pause acquisition immediately, follow the incident runbook, communicate facts, and resume only after verification.
- **Product Hunt not featured:** continue replying and measure activation; do not relaunch or manufacture engagement.
- **Reddit removal:** do not repost. Read the reason, contact moderators once if unclear, and continue participating without promotion.
- **Traffic without activation:** pause distribution, watch installs, fix the exact funnel stage.
- **More accepted beta users than capacity:** keep Free open, place high-touch applications in the next dated batch, and give a clear response time.
- **Low application count but healthy direct signups:** this is not failure; the beta is research capacity, not the product funnel.
